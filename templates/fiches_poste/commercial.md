# Job Description: Sales Executive / Account Executive

## General information
- **Title:** Account Executive
- **Department:** Sales
- **Reports to:** Head of Sales
- **Location:** Paris / Hybrid
- **Employment type:** Permanent

## Main mission
Grow the company's customer portfolio by identifying new opportunities, qualifying prospects, and leading sales cycles through signature.

## Responsibilities
- Prospect and qualify new accounts
- Lead sales cycles (discovery, demo, proposal, negotiation, closing)
- Keep the CRM up to date (pipeline, forecast, activities)
- Collaborate with Product and Customer Success teams
- Meet quarterly revenue targets
- Participate in professional events and trade shows

## Tools
- **CRM:** Salesforce / HubSpot
- **Prospecting:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Lemlist
- **Communication:** Slack, Google Workspace
- **Signature:** DocuSign

## Requirements
- 2+ years of B2B sales experience (SaaS preferred)
- Excellent communication and negotiation skills
- Proficiency with a CRM
- Results-oriented and resilient
- Fluent English

## 90-day goals
1. **30 days:** Master the offering and shadow 10 customer meetings
2. **60 days:** Lead your first sales cycles
3. **90 days:** Close your first deals
