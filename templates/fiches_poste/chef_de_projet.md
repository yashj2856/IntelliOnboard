# Job Description: Project Manager

## General information
- **Title:** Project Manager
- **Department:** PMO
- **Reports to:** PMO Director
- **Location:** Paris / Hybrid (3 days office, 2 remote)
- **Employment type:** Permanent

## Main mission
Lead the company's projects end to end by coordinating teams, managing budgets and schedules, and communicating with stakeholders to ensure on-time delivery and achievement of objectives.

## Responsibilities
- Define project scope, schedule, and budget
- Coordinate the technical and business teams involved
- Run steering committees and progress reviews
- Identify and manage project risks
- Report to leadership
- Manage relationships with internal and external stakeholders
- Document decisions and deliverables
- Contribute to continuous improvement of project methods

## Tools
- **Project management:** Jira / Linear
- **Documentation:** Confluence / Notion
- **Communication:** Slack, Google Workspace
- **Presentations:** Google Slides, Loom
- **Budget tracking:** Google Sheets

## Requirements
- 3+ years of project management experience (IT/digital preferred)
- Strong knowledge of Agile and/or waterfall methodologies
- Excellent communication and synthesis skills
- Ability to manage multiple projects in parallel
- Good understanding of technical challenges
- Certifications are a plus (PMP, PRINCE2, PSM)

## 90-day goals
1. **30 days:** Understand the project portfolio and shadow a steering committee
2. **60 days:** Lead a first project independently
3. **90 days:** Run a steering committee and propose process improvements
